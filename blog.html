Important Things to Know Before Renting a Car in France

Publié le 11 Septembre 2026 | Par l'équipe autoXeurope

Renting a car in France can be a convenient and flexible way to travel, whether you are visiting major cities, exploring the countryside, or planning a long-distance trip. Customers should carefully check what is included in the rental price and whether extras such as GPS, child seats, additional drivers, or cross-border travel involve additional fees. Understanding the pickup and return procedures is equally important, especially when collecting or returning a vehicle outside normal opening hours.
