CAR RENTAL IN PARIS - CITY OF ROMANCE

Steeped in history, cultural and architectural gems, Paris is a city to turn heads. Along with many interesting places to see and visit, Paris has a cultural heritage hard to match - which makes it one of the most visited cities in the world. For those visiting for the first time, it's understandably the Eiffel Tower that many head to. It is without a doubt the most symbolic landmark in Paris.

Art is also high on every visitor's list. The city has an abundance of rich museums, and the magnificent Notre Dame. Parisian suburbs are colourful, vibrant and interesting to see and experience - specifically Montmartre, Marais, Belleville and Trocadero - not forgetting the Latin Quarter. Few cities portray such admiration, intrigue, fascination and passion - it's no wonder Paris is considered the city of love.

Street performers, writers and artists have all placed their mark on the city along the centuries. The city's splendid avenues often hide a labyrinth of bistro eateries, restaurants and quaint coffee houses. Consider a car rental in Paris and uncover much of the urban surroundings too. Head to other magical French cities such as Lyon, Nantes and Nice, on the French Riviera.
