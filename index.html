Frequently Asked Questions

How old do I need to be to rent a car in Paris?

To rent a car in Paris, you must be at least 21 years old and have held your license for at least 1 year. Specific car categories or luxury vehicles may have restrictions and require drivers to be at least 25 years old. All drivers under 25 should check if a young driver surcharge is included in their rental rate.

Can I rent a car without a credit card in Paris?

It is necessary to have a credit card to rent a car in Paris. When you arrive at the rental counter to pick up your vehicle, you are required to present a valid credit card in the name of the primary driver indicated on your voucher. Additionally, the card must have enough available credit to cover the security deposit.

What car rental insurance is included in Paris?

Travelers picking up their rental car in Paris should know that all drivers must carry unlimited third-party liability insurance. With Auto Europe, the cost is already included in our daily rental rates. This protects travelers from injury, death, and property damage to others while behind the wheel of their car. While some major credit cards offer cardholders car rental insurance, not all car rental suppliers will accept this.
Check with Auto Europe or your supplier before your trip to determine what coverage you need and where you will purchase it. Insurance purchased at the car rental counter can be more expensive than purchasing it at pick-up time. Learn more about required and optional rental car insurance in Paris with Auto Europe.

Do I need an International Driving Permit to rent a car in in Paris?

It is strongly recommended that you carry an International Driving Permit (IDP) when driving a rental car in Paris. However, you may drive with a valid driver's license if it is accompanied by a notarized translation in French.

Do I need to pay tolls when driving my car rental in Paris?

Yes, any tolls that you may encounter in Paris and throughout France will have to be paid.
