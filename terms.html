1. Data Protection

EuropeXauto values your privacy. All personal data submitted during booking is encrypted and securely processed in accordance with European GDPR laws.

2. Reservations

Generally, reservations via EuropeXauto are confirmed immediately. Exceptions can include last-minute bookings, on request bookings, special vehicles, and optional equipment. In these cases EuropeXauto has to request a confirmation from the car rental company. Following confirmation and payment, a car rental voucher is issued to the customer. The actual rental agreement is concluded locally between the customer and the car rental company.

3. Payment

Customer has to pay in full to EuropeXauto at time of reservation. Payment must be made by credit card. Only upon receipt of full payment EuropeXauto issues a rental voucher to the customer. All prices are based on 24-hour periods starting with the pick-up time. At the time of pick-up, a physical credit card in the name of the main driver must be presented to the car rental company for deposit.

4. Deposit

At time of pick-up the car rental company requires a deposit. This takes place by an amount being blocked or charged on the international valid credit card in the name of the renter. Debit cards are generally not accepted unless specified in rental supplier conditions.

5. Car Category

Bookings are confirmed for car categories and not specific vehicle models, unless specifically stated.

6. Pick up & Return of Rental Cars

Pick-up and return times must be strictly adhered to. Late returns may incur local supplier charges at daily rates.
